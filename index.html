About

The Artist

Songs Guy is an independent instrumental artist crafting lo-fi soundscapes and ambient music for listeners who need a moment to breathe. Each track is an invitation to slow down.

With releases spanning cozy lo-fi collections to introspective singles, the music finds its home in study sessions, late evenings, and quiet mornings — wherever focus and calm are needed most.

Available on all major streaming platforms. New music released regularly.
